# SportMatchr vision

## North star

Life gets quieter as we get older — work, fewer friends, less free time, less competition. People still want to move, meet, and feel something.

**SportMatchr exists so regular people can find someone nearby and play** — competitive games *and* gentle movement (tai chi, outdoor yoga).

The long-run dream: build a real network and a real business so we can **fly regular athletes** around the world to compete and experience life differently — not just pros, not just people with money and connections.

That dream is the destination. It is **not** what we ship in the UI today.

---

## Dream vs Now

| | Destination (dream) | Start (now) |
|---|---|---|
| Job | Change how regular athletes experience the world | Get strangers meeting this week at free places |
| Geography | Worldwide, city by city | **Ottawa** (prove-it) → Montreal & Toronto next |
| Sport | Many sports + gentle movement | **Every activity** — volleyball, shinny, cricket, tai chi… |
| Money | Fund travel, events, maybe courts | Prove people show up |
| Partners | Venues, events, court building | Free public places we map |
| Product feel | Opportunity and adventure | Near you · free place · join · come back |

**Guardrail:** Keep Phase 1 looking like Phase 1. Travel, sponsorships, athlete recruiting, venue deals, and “build new courts” stay in this document until density and money exist. Do **not** launch empty multi-city maps.

---

## Phase order (do not skip)

1. **Local play** — alone / bored → pick any activity → free place near you → meet in public → play / move  
2. **Density + habit** — multi-sport hosts, crews, rematch, streaks that mean you showed up  
3. **Business that pays** — monetize only after usage is real  
4. **Fly regular athletes** — travel and compete; experience life differently  
5. **Venues, events, maybe new courts** — after people are already involved  
6. **Next Canadian cities** — franchise the *method* to Montreal & Toronto, then further — not a feature dump  

---

## Building now (with what we have)

- Expo app: Home, Now (Online / Looking / Games), Map, Sessions, Profile, Create, chat  
- Location: near-you distances, free-first places, coarsened Go Live presence  
- Soft trust: **real face photo required**, SafetyGate, public places only  
- Multi-activity Ottawa free places (courts, fields, rinks, lawns)  
- Seed ops: [SEED_PLAYBOOK.md](./SEED_PLAYBOOK.md)  

**Winning the start** (30–60 days, Ottawa):

1. Someone opens the app, picks *their* activity, sees a free place or real meetup near them  
2. They join / message without hand-holding  
3. At least one meetup happens without the founder there (**any** sport/activity)  
4. Someone comes back a second time  

Then: expand hard to **Montreal and Toronto**.

## Future wishlist (not this sprint)

### Travel & regular athletes
- Fund flights / lodging for regular athletes to compete in other cities  
- Scrimmages and trips that feel like a different life  

### Venues (after people)
- Partner with staffed venues once recurring sessions are full  

### Events

### Courts & places
- Maybe help build or improve places where demand outstrips supply

- Montreal & Toronto free-place datasets + local hosts

### Money (later)

Ottawa is the gym (~1.5M metro — enough to learn). Montreal and Toronto are the arena. Expanding is the plan; empty multi-city launch is the trap. Every sport gets people; every sport needs free places — including tai chi and outdoor yoga for older adults and beginners.
