# SportMatchr seed playbook (Ottawa · multi-activity · free places)

Prove the loop in **Ottawa** first (metro is big enough). Then clone to **Montreal & Toronto**.

Target: **hosts across many activities**, **50–100+ users**, free places for every sport — including tai chi & outdoor yoga.

## Success test (30–60 days) — any activity counts

1. Open app → pick a sport/activity → see a **free place** + real person/session within ~5 km  
2. Join / message without founder hand-holding  
3. At least one meetup happens **without the founder there** (volleyball, shinny, tai chi — all count)  
4. Someone comes back a second time  

## Multi-sport hosts (not pickleball-only)

Recruit **parallel hosts** — every sport gets people:

| Activity | Where to find hosts | Free place examples |
|----------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Beach / volleyball | Beach groups, Carleton, Meetup | Mooney’s Bay, Westboro Beach |
| Basketball | Park crews, campus | Brewer, Lansdowne courts |
| Soccer / ultimate | Rec leagues, Facebook | Fields at Brewer, Vincent Massey |
| Shinny (winter) | Rink boards, hockey groups | City outdoor rinks |
| Badminton / tennis / pickleball | Clubs (invite to *public* courts) | Public courts |
| Cricket / baseball | Diamond communities | Neighbourhood diamonds, Brewer |
| Running | Run crews | Canal / park meet points |
| Outdoor yoga / tai chi | Libraries, seniors centres, community associations | Quiet lawns, Major’s Hill, canal greens |

**Week 1–2**

1. Line up **at least 6–8 hosts across ≥4 different activities**  
2. Each creates one **recurring** free-place meetup  
3. You attend early sessions and fill spots manually  

**Week 2–4 — users**

1. Post in r/ottawa, local FB groups, campus boards, sport-specific Discords — **per sport**, not one pickleball pitch  
2. Pitch: “Find people for volleyball, shinny, cricket, tai chi… at **free public places** in Ottawa — not dating.”  
3. Goal: **75+ accounts**; verified-only stays **OFF** while feed is thin

- [ ] One unaided meetup + one return visit (see below)  

## Unaided meetup test (founder ops)

Prove strangers meet **without you in the chat or at the place**, then return:

1. Seed ≥2 hosts in different activities with recurring free-place sessions  
2. Recruit players via sport-specific channels — you may intro, then **leave**  
3. Confirm in-app: Join → chat → meetup happens with founder **absent**  
4. Confirm ≥1 player opens the app again within 7 days and joins/hosts again  
5. Log date + activity + place in founder notes — that’s the gate to expand  

Until step 3–4 pass, do **not** market Montreal/Toronto as live cities.

## City sequencing

1. **Ottawa** — prove-it city (gym)  
2. **Montreal & Toronto** — expand hard after unaided density (biggest Canadian markets)  
3. Later: Calgary, Vancouver, etc.  

Do **not** open empty TO/MTL maps that show “0 near you.” Optional: draft place lists in the background (`constants/cities/`).

## Do not

- Bet the company on one paddle sport  
- Launch Toronto/Montreal as empty shells before Ottawa works  
- Stay Ottawa-only forever (too small as *end state*)  
- Turn verified-only ON while Looking is empty  
- Lead with paid clubs over free public places
