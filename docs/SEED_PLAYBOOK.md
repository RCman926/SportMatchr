# SportMatchr seed playbook (Glebe / Centretown · Pickleball)

Do this before adding features. Target: **50–100 real users** and **3 recurring hosts** in the wedge pocket.

## Success test (30–60 days)

1. Someone opens the app and sees a real person/session within ~5 km
2. They join/message without you holding their hand
3. At least one game happens without you there
4. Someone comes back a second time

## Week 1 — hosts

1. Recruit **3 hosts** who already play pickleball near Lansdowne / Glebe / Brewer
2. Each host creates one **recurring** session (e.g. Tue 7pm, Sat 10am)
3. You attend the first two yourself and fill empty spots manually (text friends)

## Week 2–3 — users

1. Post in local Facebook groups, Reddit (r/ottawa), campus boards, pickleball clubs
2. Pitch: “Find people for pickleball at Lansdowne — public courts only”
3. Goal: **75 accounts** with phone verify preferred (not required to join early)
4. Prefer people who live/work in Glebe, Centretown, Old Ottawa South, Carleton

## Ops checklist

- [ ] 3 recurring sessions live on the calendar
- [ ] 50+ users in pocket
- [ ] Verified-only still OFF on Solos
- [ ] No new sports/cities until Tuesday open play fills without you
- [ ] After first real games: rematch + next-week + show-up tracking

## Do not

- Expand to Gatineau / Kanata / “all Ottawa Valley” for seeding
- Add tournaments, sponsors, or athlete recruiting
- Turn verified-only ON while the feed is empty
